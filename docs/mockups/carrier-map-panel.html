Carrier map and carrier management mockup, revision 1, 2026-10-07

Two normal panels. The carrier map is a radar picture fixed on the ship, always north-up, with range rings (the 10 nm ring always), the final bearing, the marshal stack, the bolter pattern and the recovery tanker. The carrier management panel is where BRC, final bearing, weather, the recovery tanker and the marshal position are set or overridden; everything else (the map, the recovery board) only shows them. Sample: CVN-72, Case III. Side numbers and values are invented.

Map

Fixed on the ship and always north-up; the wheel changes the range, the centre never moves. The 10 nm ring is always drawn and labelled; the other rings follow the range (spacing in the map's Settings tab). The ship symbol points along its BRC. Aircraft are labelled by side number and altitude in hundreds of feet; colour only for a problem (amber low state, red at bingo).

Case I

The marshal stack and the final-bearing line give way to the port holding circle (left-hand, tangent to the BRC, ship at 3 o'clock, at most 5 nm across) and the initial 3 nm astern (CV NATOPS 6.2.1). The 10 nm ring stays.

Management

Each value says where it comes from (DCS, a default, or a person). Typing a value overrides it; "Use DCS" or "Use default" puts the automatic value back. The recovery board and the map read these values and never edit them.
