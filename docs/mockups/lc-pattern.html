A click on P/S steps PAT → STR → empty; on OPT T&G → FS → LA → empty. Whoever holds the Strip sets them: AP before the transfer (the cells are on AP's arrivals too), LC after. A drag between lc-arrivals, lc-pattern, lc-final and lc-landed does the other legal step when the pilot changes his mind.
