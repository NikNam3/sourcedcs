Transit through an active MOA mockup, lane U-MOA-TRANSIT, ADR 0127 (Proposed)

Decisions H148 and H128. A MOA in use: the side that separates its users declines the transit or accepts it with one altitude release (a block for the users, a band for the transit, ≥ 1,000 ft apart, JO 7110.65BB ¶4-5-1 a; JO 7610.14A ¶8-1-9 b.1), and ends it with Resume ops. Everything is grey unless something needs doing (dark cockpit). Names and numbers are illustrative: BRAVO MOA, 5,000 ft–FL280, controlling CTR, users VIPER11/12 worked by CTR on the MOA frequency, the transit BLADE31 worked by AP.
