PAR and Marshal stack panels mockups, lane PANELS-DESIGN, 2026-10-05

The request: "PAR needs the actual PAR panel, and the marshal stack should also have its own marshal stack panel, the strip bay shouldn't have to order them." Two dock panels for crc-desktop, each beside the EFSP Strip panel. Static mockups with sample data; the research, sources, data plan, build plan and open questions are in docs/wip/PANELS-DESIGN.md.

PAR panel (revision 4) →

A normal panel, placed wherever the controller wants. Elevation and azimuth scopes against range from touchdown, zoomable on both axes with touchdown fixed in the corner, an optional autoscale, with the glidepath, centreline, safety limits, DA and a short plot trail. Dark cockpit: all grey while the approach is normal; amber, orange-red or red only when something needs attention. Landing assured / Missed approach.

  • Interactive: pick a situation, fly a sample approach, compare five track styles, zoom the scopes
  • Removed after review: docking picture, required-calls list, suggested call, cadence bar, coloured bands, "need" vertical speed
  • Needs a server-computed PAR sample: the wire altitude is rounded to 100 ft and today's FINAL measures to the radar site, not the threshold

Marshal / recovery board (revision 7) →

Every recovering aircraft by side number in landing order (SEQ, SIDE, TYPE, FUEL:EAT, ANG, STATUS) beside a Case III picture drawn after the CV NATOPS / Hornet checklist profile: ship at the top, the final bearing with DME marks (0–12 nm to scale, compressed out to the marshal fixes), the bolter/waveoff racetrack, and the marshal stack along the marshal radial from 21 DME. The Marshal sets the radial; the header shows the stack position (radial and DME range). TO GO at the foot. Dark cockpit: amber for low fuel, past EAT, an unpassed clearance change; red at bingo or a foul deck.

  • Interactive: situations (including a bolter), drag HOLD rows, Delta, Commence, Back to stack
  • Needs from the server: pushedAt, lane in the view, a Delta op, deck state (not modelled yet)

Carrier map and carrier management (revision 5) →

The carrier map: fixed on the ship, always north-up, range rings with the 10 nm ring always, final bearing, the marshal stack on its radial, the recovery tanker's racetrack, aircraft by side number and altitude; Case I shows port holding and the initial instead. The carrier management panel sets or overrides BRC, final bearing, Case, ramp time, marshal radial and first fix, weather and the recovery tanker; everything else only reads them.

  • Interactive: edit any value, reset to the automatic one, zoom the map, switch Case
