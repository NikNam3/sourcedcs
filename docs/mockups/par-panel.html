  1. 1Approach header new server[SOURCE-DEFINED]

    The approach the controller is working and its geometry. Runway end, GPA, TCH, DA and the final approach course come from a new parApproaches Facility entry; AZ/EL show whether the PAR sample has azimuth and elevation.

  2. 2Flight existsnew server

    Callsign, type and beacon from the Strip and the correlated track. GS and VS are the contact's own (DCS velocity); "need" is the rate the glidepath wants at this ground speed (GS × 101.3 × tan GPA).

  3. 3Range from touchdown new server

    Measured from the runway threshold, not the approach radar site (today's FINAL measures to the airfield reference point).

  4. 4Required calls exists

    From JO 7110.65 5-10 and 5-12: the event calls light amber when due and strike through once passed. Computed from the sample; the controller never ticks anything.

  5. 5Elevation and azimuth scopes new server[SOURCE-DEFINED]

    Range on x with touchdown at the left, a mark every mile. Elevation: glidepath (green), ON band (darker), SLIGHTLY band, the lower safety limit (red dashes), the DA tick. Azimuth: course centreline, bands, and the safety wedge. The white square is the latest plot, the dots are the last 12 plots (1 s apart), so the trend is visible. Range scale 10 / 5 NM, auto 5 inside 4 NM.

  6. 6Deviation readouts new server[SOURCE-DEFINED]

    Angular deviation (what the bands are in) and the same in feet, the band word, and altitude and track. Angles about the glidepath apex, so bands narrow towards touchdown like a real PAR's cursors.

  7. 7Suggested call exists

    The 5-12-4 sentence for this plot: band + trend for elevation and azimuth, and the heading to fly. Advice for the controller's voice; nothing is transmitted. Red when 5-10-14 applies (safety limit before DA, contact lost).

  8. 8Cadence exists

    The existing 5-second metronome off the mission clock ("approximately every 5 seconds", 5-12-4).

  9. 9Terminal buttons exists

    Unchanged from ADR 0075: Landing assured (to BALL), Missed approach (to par-missed). On a carrier lane: Ball and Waveoff.

States that matter

Compact cards (header, rail and buttons hidden). Same flight, same approach, different moments.

What interacting does

GestureEffect
none (watching)Everything updates once a second from the server's PAR sample. The suggested call and the due calls change by themselves; the controller talks.
Range 10 / 5Client-only scale toggle; auto 5 NM inside 4 NM, a manual choice holds until the next aircraft.
Landing assuredExisting op: FINAL → BALL (PAR), the Strip leaves par-final; the panel says "No aircraft on final".
Missed approachExisting op: FINAL → BOLTER_WAVEOFF, Strip to par-missed. The panel offers the published missed approach text (from config) as the call.
Hover / tap a trail dotShows that plot's time, range and deviations (client-only).
Rotate to PAR (SFA)Existing op elsewhere; when it lands, the panel picks the new aircraft up and starts its trail fresh.

Carrier lanes (CV_APP1 / CV_APP2)

The same panel with kind CARRIER: Mode III talk-down to the SPN-46 picture (CV NATOPS 6.4.7.8). Touchdown moves with the ship (hull touchdownOffsetM, new server), the course is the final bearing (BRC − 9°), the glideslope 3.5° [SOURCE-DEFINED], altitudes spoken as angels, headings as soft corrections ("right five"), the required calls become lock-on, approaching tipover, each mile, at minimums, and the terminals Ball / Waveoff.

Mockup only. Band thresholds (ON ±0.2°/±0.5°, SLIGHTLY to 0.5°/1.5°, safety −0.7° / ±2.5°), the 3 s no-plot alarm and the 12-plot trail are proposals [SOURCE-DEFINED]; no public source gives numbers for "slightly", "well" or the safety lines.
