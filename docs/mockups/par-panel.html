PAR panel mockup, revision 2, 2026-10-05

A normal crc-desktop panel, opened from Panels like the EFSP or Settings and placed wherever the controller wants. It shows the precision approach radar picture for the aircraft on final and nothing the controller already knows. When the approach is normal the panel is all grey. Colour appears only when something needs attention.

Try the scopes

Wheel: zoom range (both scopes together). Shift + wheel: zoom height or lateral offset (this scope only). Drag: pan. Double-click: back to the default window.

What changed from revision 1

Gone: the docking picture, "need −750", the required-calls list, the suggested call, the cadence bar and the coloured bands. Scopes are grey traces on the panel's own background. Deviation numbers are plain text unless they are a problem.

When colour appears

ColourMeansWhere on this panel
greyNormal. The controller reads it when needed.Everything while the aircraft is within tolerance, including "slightly" off: the plot shows it and the controller says it.
amberLook at this now.Azimuth or elevation past the "well" tolerance; no radar plot for 3 s.
orange-redSomething failed.Elevation or azimuth data unavailable; that scope is blanked with the reason.
redPast a safety limit.Below the lower safety limit, or outside the azimuth safety limit, before DA.

The tolerances are ours: no public source gives numbers for "slightly", "well" or the safety lines. Proposed: on glidepath within ±0.2°, "well" beyond 0.5°, lower safety limit 0.7° below; on course within ±0.5°, "well" beyond 1.5°, safety limit ±2.5°. They would live in a tuning file and need your sign-off.
