A normal crc-desktop panel. It shows the precision approach radar picture for the aircraft on final and nothing the controller already knows. When the approach is normal the panel is all grey except the track. Colour elsewhere appears only when something needs attention.

Zoom

Touchdown stays in the bottom-left corner of the elevation scope and on the left edge of the azimuth scope, always. Wheel: zoom range (both scopes). Shift + wheel: zoom height or lateral offset (this scope). No panning.

Autoscale

With the setting on, the window follows the aircraft so the track sits at the golden-ratio point (61.8 % of the range width, likewise in height) until the window is down to 10 NM; from there it stays at 10 NM. A manual zoom pauses autoscale (the window label says so); double-click resumes it. With the setting off the window stays where the controller put it.

Track styles

B to E give the track its own colour and dim the reference lines so the plot stands out. Amber and red stay reserved for problems, so a coloured track turns amber or red only when it is one.

When colour appears

ColourMeansWhere on this panel
greyNormal.Everything while the aircraft is within tolerance, including "slightly" off.
magentaThe aircraft (style E, approved).The current plot and its trail.
amberLook at this now.No radar plot for 3 s.
orange-redSomething failed.Elevation or azimuth data unavailable; that scope is blanked with the reason.
redOutside tolerance.Elevation or azimuth outside its tolerance (default ±1°), above DA only. Below DA the pilot decides and nothing is coloured.

Defaults: glideslope 3.06°, tolerance ±1° in elevation and azimuth, set per controller in the PAR tab of the Settings panel. The dashed tolerance lines start at DA, because they do not apply below it.
