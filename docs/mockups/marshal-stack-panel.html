Reading the board

FUEL:EAT is the fuel state in thousands of pounds and the EAT minute, as on the real board. ANG is the holding altitude; DME is not shown because it is always angels + 15. STATUS is HOLD, COMM (commenced), APP A / APP B (the approach lane) or TRAP. SEQ is the landing order. TO GO counts the aircraft still to recover.

Try it

Drag a HOLD row to re-sequence: the flights whose angels or EAT changed show those values amber until you click the row to say the new clearance was passed. Commence sits on the next flight to push. The pattern on the right places commenced and approaching aircraft by DME from the radar picture.

Kept from the real board, changed for ours

Kept: landing order, FUEL:EAT, the approach schematic, TO GO, the header strip. Changed: the yellow and orange text on normal values is grey here; the green bar (deck status) shows only when the deck is foul, and needs deck state from the server, which is not modelled yet.
