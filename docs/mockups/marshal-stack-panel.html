Marshal stack panel mockup, revision 2, 2026-10-06

A normal crc-desktop panel for CV_MARSHAL (read-only at the other carrier Positions). It shows the stack the way it is flown, top slot up, so the Strip Bay can go back to holding Strips without ordering them. The Marshal knows the procedures; the panel keeps the numbers and shows only what has gone wrong or changed. Sample: CVN-72, Case III, Charlie 2040Z. Callsigns and fuel are invented.

Try it

Drag a holding flight to another slot: everyone it passes gets new angels, DME and EAT, marked amber until you click the row to say the new clearance has been passed. "Commence" sits on the next flight to push.

What changed from revision 1

Gone: the docking picture, the numbered annotations, the marshal-instructions box, the read-back checklist, the push countdown and the coloured row backgrounds. The ladder is grey; amber and red mark only the problems below.
