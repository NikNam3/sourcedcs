Marshal stack panel mockup, lane PANELS-DESIGN, 2026-10-05

A dock panel for CV_MARSHAL (read-only at the other carrier Positions): the Case III stack as a time-and-altitude ladder, so the cv-marshal-stack Bay can go back to holding Strips. Every gesture is an existing stack op (ADR 0064 / 0074); the panel adds no arithmetic: angels, DME and EAT come from the server's derived stack. Research and data plan: docs/wip/PANELS-DESIGN.md.

  1. 1Ship header exists

    Hull, Case (PriFly sets it; read-only here), BRC, final bearing, altimeter, mission clock. Same values as today's banner.

  2. 2Stack settings existsnew server

    Marshal radial and Charlie are the existing SetMarshalRadial / SetCharlieTime inputs, moved out of the Bay. NEXT PUSH counts down on the mission clock. Delta… is a proposed op: Charlie + N minutes (even, ≥ 4) for holding flights (CV NATOPS 6.4.9).

  3. 3The ladder existsnew server

    Highest slot at the top, as the stack is flown. ANGELS (6 + slot), DME (angels + 15) and EAT (Charlie + slot minutes) are the server's derived values; the slot is the only thing the Marshal sets. A vacant slot shows the server's preview faintly. Pushed flights keep their slot (push never renumbers); their push time is the stored pushedAt.

  4. 4Fuel against bingo existsnew server[SOURCE-DEFINED]

    State (thousands of lb) over bingo, and how old the report is. Amber when within 1,000 lb of bingo; a dashed age after 10 minutes means "update state" (air ops asks about every ten minutes).

  5. 5Push and Commence existsnew server

    The bottom holding flight gets the push countdown and, inside 15 s, a Commence button to its lane (APP1/APP2 alternate by push order, assignLanes).

  6. 6Marshal instructions exists

    For the selected flight, the ten items of CV NATOPS 6.4.2 in order, from the derived stack and the flight's carrier fields: ready to read.

  7. 7Re-clear list

    Appears when an op changes holding flights' angels, DME or EAT (a move, a close-up, Charlie, Delta, a Case change). Ordered top of the stack first, the order NATOPS gives for new EATs.

  8. 8Commenced exists

    Pushed flights until the lane says Radar contact; then the flight is the PAR panel's (FINAL).

5. Handed to approach, then to final

A commenced flight belongs to its lane (CV_APP1/2 own COMMENCED). The panel keeps it in the commenced list, read-only, so the Marshal sees the flow. When the lane taps Radar contact (the APPROACH_TO_FINAL handoff) the flight leaves this panel and appears in that lane's PAR panel. A bolter or waveoff (drag to the Bolter Bay) returns to final, not to the stack.
