## What the usage guide should say (§8F "Alert and scramble")

- **ALERT (14E)** is on DEPARTURE Strips at OPS, CD, GND and TWR. Pick `NONE`, `ALERT` or `SCRAMBLE`.
  OPS sets it while it holds the Strip; after that, whoever owns the Strip sets it. `ALERT` shows
  only in the field.
- **SCRAMBLE** raises a red line at the top of the Strip panel on every tab, for every controller:
  `SCRAMBLE VIPER11 INCIRLIK · CLEARED · <access route> constrained · N flagged`. Click the callsign
  to jump to the Strip.
- The scrambling Strip gets a red `SCRAMBLE` chip. Every departure at PUSHBACK, TAXI, RUNWAY_QUEUE
  or LUAW, and every arrival at LANDED or TAXI_IN, at the same field gets an amber `SCRAMBLE` chip
  and a reason line telling it to keep clear of the access route.
- **Nothing is reordered, held or moved.** Sequencing is the controller's call (the guide's
  `[GAP]`). The flags are SOURCE practice, not FAA doctrine.
- The indication ends by itself when the scrambler is DEPARTED (or dropped). To cancel on the
  ground, set 14E back to `ALERT` or `NONE`. The field is never reset automatically.
