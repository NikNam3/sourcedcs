Pattern board, FINAL, and SFA frequency racks

L18 client half, for approval. Three panels over two shared components. The server half (the three Positions, SFA rotation) waits for L17's Roles. Every colour is a CSS variable, nothing is colour-only (border, text and position carry state too).

1. RSU: the pattern board

RSU is supervisory and has no separation authority (guide 4.1 rule 4). Its Bay is a board of four legs, not a rack of Strips. A leg is a Rack (ADR 0064: legs are racks, not states), so moving an aircraft between legs is the existing Move gesture, by drag. The same component serves PriFly (legs initial, break, downwind, groove), configured by leg list.

  1. Four columns from the Bay's rack list. A chip is callsign, type, intent (T&G / full stop / low approach) and minutes in the pattern, from the mission clock.
  2. Drag a chip to another leg (or tap its leg name, for touch). It is a Move, nothing else.
  3. Two buttons per chip: Landed (state RECOVERED) and Drop. Nothing to type.
  4. Advisories, never refusals: 2+ on the final leg, a chip in the pattern over 10 min, a runway that is closed or suspended with aircraft in the pattern. They are amber tags under the board; RSU has no authority, so nothing is blocked.
  5. The CLOSED leg is the closed (overhead) pattern, kept as a leg because the guide names it.

2. PAR and the carrier Final: the FINAL component

One component, no inputs (guide 7.10). Four large figures, a 5-second cadence bar, a call prompt, two terminal buttons. It holds one Strip. Nothing in it is typed.

  1. Figures: runway (or deck for the carrier), decision altitude, distance to touchdown, and glidepath deviation when the track gives one. All derived.
  2. Cadence bar is a free-running 5 s metronome from the mission clock. It is a cue for the talk-down rhythm; it is not tied to taps, because a voice call cannot be observed and a tap per call is data entry.
  3. Call prompt: appears when a required call falls due (every mile, glidepath interception, decision altitude, trend deviation) and clears itself after 4 s.
  4. Terminal buttons: PAR says Landing assured and Missed approach; the carrier says Ball and Waveoff. Both map onto the existing FINAL states (BALL, BOLTER_WAVEOFF), so no new state is added. They are the only gestures.

3. SFA: frequencies, not Positions

The controller moves, the Strip's frequency never does (guide 4.7, D17). The board has one rack per pool frequency. Each shows who is on it now and who is next in the rotation. APP holds jurisdiction. The rotation is one button: Rotate onto 3rd freq.

  1. Pool of at least five UHF frequencies from facility config ([SOURCE-DEFINED]); three are in the rotation at a time.
  2. The Strip shows on 288.3. That text is the Strip's frequency and does not change on a rotation transfer.
  3. What the transfer changes is which controller is on that frequency: ownership moves, the Strip's frequency stays. The UI says "SFA rotated: RAPCON to GCA on 288.3", not "contact 288.3".
