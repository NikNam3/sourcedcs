## What the usage guide should say

An overflight is created `INBOUND` (CTR or APP). **Radar Contact** puts it `IN_SECTOR`. From there, either **Leaves Sector** (it goes to an agency we do not model; the Strip becomes `HANDED_OFF`, still yours, then **Drop**), or **Coordinate -> HANDOFF** to the next Facility (CTR -> APP): the Strip arrives `INBOUND` in the receiver's overflight Bay once accepted, and yours becomes `HANDED_OFF` (Drop). POINT_OUT and TRAFFIC work from `IN_SECTOR` and change no state. An `INBOUND` or `HANDED_OFF` overflight cannot coordinate. `docs/efsp-usage-guide.md` still says `TRANSITING` (not edited: shared).

## What the briefing should say

F14 (an overflight can never propose coordination) is closed. Overflight states are the guide's four. The soak's `overflight` script now hands half CTR -> APP.
