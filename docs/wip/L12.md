## What the usage guide should say

- §3G / ordnance: `3G` (ORDNANCE) is on TWR's, APP's and CTR's Strips and on every mission line
  (H55). Other Positions reach it in the expanded view (▼). Any Position the pilot is talking to
  records `HUNG` there.
- When a flight's ordnance is `HUNG`, every Position's Strip for it shows an amber **HUNG** chip and
  one reason line: *"Hung ordnance. SOURCE practice: after landing, taxi to Hot cargo pad; the runway
  is the controller's call. Runway 05/23 (05) assigned."* On a departure the line says "if it
  returns". The chip is advice. It never disables NLA, and the flight lands, taxis and drops as usual.
- The runway named is whatever the Strip resolves to: its runway rack, then 8A/8B, then the active
  end. The line never recommends one, because the pad is a placeholder name with no position (H21).
- Set `CLEAN` or `EXPENDED` when the pilot reports the store safe; the chip goes.
- Until client field state lands (L1b), the line reads *"The hot cargo pad is shown when field
  state is available."*

## Walks (briefing §6 step 4)

`efsp-scenario-ordnance.test.mjs`:
- **Arrival, reported inbound to APP (H55):** APP picks HUNG on its own grid, and the chip and line
  appear. NLA goes to TWR, and TWR's Strip shows the same chip. FINAL → LANDED → TAXI_IN are
  pressed on the real button, which is never disabled. At GND, the expanded view offers `3G` →
  CLEAN, and the chip and line go.
- **"Request runway 23 for hot cargo":** TWR edits 8B to `23`. The line re-states
  `Runway 05/23 (23) assigned.` and still recommends nothing (asserted with a regex).
- **"Ordnance safe now":** CLEAN (arrival walk) and EXPENDED (runway-23 walk) both clear the chip.
- **A departure that aborts and returns with a hung store:** OPS sets HUNG from the expanded view,
  and the line reads "if it returns, taxi to Hot cargo pad". The walk uses an OPS-proposed DEPARTURE
  Strip rather than flying an abort, because the Role alone decides the wording. A queued departure
  with HUNG is in the server scenario.
- **Mission line (H55):** TAC_C2 sets HUNG on a MISSION Strip bound to an inbound. The ATC twin is
  advised and the MISSION Strip is not (server scenario; reachability covers the TAC_C2/AIC/GCI/JTAC
  field).
- **A runway change** (05 → 23, with its acknowledgements and inspection) re-states the runway, and
  the pad does not move (server scenario).
- **Hung ordnance does not reorder a runway queue** (server scenario).
