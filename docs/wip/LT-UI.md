## Defaults taken

1. **LT-Q3 (T10, map → Strip):** kept "click a contact on the map selects its Strip", with no ring. The human has since ruled the same (H134, LT-Q3 = a), so this is now the ruling. To drop it: remove the one `selectStripForTrack` call in `map-interactions.js`.
2. **How long the "Show on map" ring stays: 8 s** (`RING_SHOW_MS`, ours). Long enough to find the contact after the pan, short enough that it never needs dismissing. Pressing Show on map again restarts it; another Strip's Show on map moves it.
3. **Time-to-find window: 30 s** (ours). The briefing's "first selection within a window after arrival". "Arrival" is taken as the Bay coming on screen, as ADR 0072 measures it; I did not move the start to the Strip entering the Bay.
4. **"(ours)" once in the header**, not after each target: every §11.5 target is ours.
5. **T26:** the calm line is grey now (was amber): monitoring two frequencies is not a problem. LUAW while combined stays coloured, with a short fact and no citation. The acknowledgement lives in the window only (not shared, lost on reload; the line comes back after a reload, which is harmless).
6. **T20:** the menu item stays "Coordinate…"; the target is named on the button that sends it. The OPERATIONAL_REQUEST verb on the button is "Request".

## Usage-guide wording (for the docs pass)

- **Show on map (§ Strip menu / correlation):** "Selecting a Strip does not mark its aircraft on the map. To find it, open the Strip's ⋯ menu and choose **Show on map**: the contact is ringed for 8 seconds and the map pans to it if it is off screen. The item is greyed out when the flight's contact is outside your radar coverage, and missing when the flight has no contact (use Bind…). Clicking a contact on the map still selects its Strip."
- **Formation split:** "A part split off a formation is an ordinary flight on the board: nothing on its Strip says it was split. The split is in the Mutation log, and Undo still reverses it."
- **Coordination:** "The send button in the Coordinate popover says what it does and to whom, for example **Hand off to CTR** or **Point out to AP**. When an arrival can go to Approach or to the carrier's Marshal, pick the target in the second list and the button follows."
- **Metrics:** "Time-to-find is the time from a Bay coming on screen to the first Strip you select in it, counted only when that selection comes within 30 seconds (ours: later than that you came back to work a Strip, not to find one). Every target in the panel is ours, from the implementation guide. A mission shorter than an hour shows one column, because its last hour is the whole mission."
- **Combination line:** "While you hold LC with another Position, a grey line above the Position tabs lists the frequencies to monitor, for example *Monitor TWR 251.0, GND 275.8*. Click it to acknowledge; it stays away until the combination changes. With a departure lined up (LUAW) while combined, the line is coloured and says so." (The JO 7210.3 ¶2-6-5b/c and ¶2-6-12 references move to the guide's background paragraph.)
- **Stereo routes:** "The stereo-route list beside **+ New Strip** is greyed out and empty when no stereo routes are installed."
- **Map labels:** no wording needed (labels now appear after login without a reload).
