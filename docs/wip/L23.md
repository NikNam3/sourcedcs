## What the usage guide should say

- **AIC workflow (H2).** TAC_C2 transfers a line in at ON_STATION. AIC annotates it, moves it
  On Station / Committed, and hands it back from the ⋯ menu ("Hand back to TAC_C2") or by dragging to
  the TAC_C2 tab. AIC cannot advance state (NLA disabled with the reason; SetState refused). AIC can
  hand a line to nobody but TAC_C2. The TOFI dialogue on an AIC-held line is TAC_C2's: TAC_C2 accepts
  CTR's exit, and a controller holding both seats answers it from the AIC tab and it is sent as TAC_C2.
- **JTAC workflow (H40).** A JTAC is sent only the lines TAC_C2 has handed it (Strips, FDRs,
  correlations, MARSA, alerts for those flights). It can annotate nothing and hand back only. It binds
  no contact and declares no MARSA. A JTAC who leaves the seat leaves the line with TAC_C2.
- **A covering Position.** Strips routed to it land in its own Bay for the line's state (else its first
  Bay implying none) and go back to the Position they came from when that Position is manned again.
- **Coordination.** The proposer can Cancel an open proposal or End an accepted exchange from the ⋯
  menu; a receiver dropping its side ends the exchange on the proposer's Strip.
- **OPS alert status.** OPS sets 14E on a departure at every state until it is DROPPED.
- A refusal now names why (`TAC_C2 holds this Strip`, `ON_STATION is not AIC's to change`).
