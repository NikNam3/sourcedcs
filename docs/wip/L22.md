# L22: the STARS scheme on ATC scopes

- **Mockup:** `docs/wip/L22-mockup.html`. The human approved it (H71), with its nine defaults.
- **Build:** ADR `docs/adr/0088-atc-scopes-draw-in-the-stars-scheme.md`. Screenshots of the four scenes, taken
  in the real app, are in `docs/wip/L22/` (e2e lane 8, `crc-desktop/e2e/l22-stars-scope.spec.js`).

## What was built

- **crc-sync**
  - `presentation.js` sends `scheme: 'TACTICAL'|'ATC'` per contact per session, with a hold of
    2 tactical sweeps. It is in `WIRE_KEYS`, and the wire-strictness test covers it.
  - `sensor-specs.json` has a new `presentation` section that classes each radar kind, and `radars.js`
    stamps the class on every radar.
  - Every Facility config has `positionLetters`, which are validated and sent in the EFSP snapshot.
  - `ws-hub.js` passes whether the session holds an ATC Position, and it re-sends the picture when that
    changes.
- **crc-desktop**
  - `atc-scope.js` is new. It works out the relation, the view, the local acknowledgements and
    click-downs, the coast state, the palette and the black-scope decision.
  - `track-label.js` gains `atcBlockLines` and its helpers.
  - `geojson.js` has an ATC branch in dots, labels, leaders and trails, and ATC tracks draw no PPL.
  - `map-setup.js` adds the `atc-targets`, `atc-symbols` and `atc-labels` layers, the target click
    and `applyAtcBackground`.
  - The settings panel gains an "ATC Map Background" row, and `efsp-state.js` stores the position letters.

## Defaults taken (P2, all from the approved mockup)

1. The black background applies only in an ATC-only session: every radar is classed `ATC` and there is
   no datalink.
2. `SA` fires only on `iffOverride === 'hostile'`.
3. The indicator text is `SA`.
4. `M` is the letter for every tactical Position, including JTAC.
5. Coast is client-only. It starts after two sweeps of the slowest ATC radar, with a 6 s fallback, and
   lasts through the existing fade window.
6. Line 0 stacks by severity: EM/RF/HJ, CA, SA, then the conformance tag.
7. A click on the target accepts or acknowledges, steps down, or expands. A click on the block opens the
   panel.
8. A provisional correlation keeps its `?`.
9. The colour and timing values are the mockup's.

Build-time defaults of my own:

- A handoff or point-out offered to me is **accepted by a click on its target**. This sends the same
  `ACCEPT` mutation the Strip's button sends.
- A blink after an accept happens only for a change this scope saw happen. A reconnect shows the
  settled picture, with no blinking for old exchanges.
- A same-Facility transfer, where TWR drags a Strip to APP with no PROPOSED step, blinks at the
  sender, as H41 said.
- Letters for the non-radar Positions are OPS `O`, CD `D` and GND `G`. They matter only if such a
  Position owns a Strip whose contact is on an ATC scope.
- The ATC text size follows the app's scale setting. `atc-scope.js` copies the tactical label layer's
  size, so `applyScale` in `app.js` stays untouched.

## What the guide should say (for whoever owns `efsp-usage-guide.md` §8F)

On Tower, Approach and Center, contacts are drawn STARS-style. The data block depends on your
relation to the flight:

- white FDB: yours;
- flashing white: being handed to you;
- yellow `PO`: pointed out to you;
- green `alt gs`: someone else's, and the letter on the blue disc says whose;
- green `code / alt` with `*`: squawking with no flight plan;
- `+` alone: primary only.

- Click the **disc** to accept a handoff or point-out, to acknowledge `EM` or `CA`, or to open or
  close a PDB.
- Click the **block** for the track panel.
- `SA` marks a contact someone has declared hostile.
- `M` means the flight is under tactical control after TOFI.
- `CST` means your correlated flight has stopped returning.
- Settings → Map Overlays → "ATC Map Background" off gives a black STARS scope when you hold only ATC
  Positions.

## Walks not done

- There is no live-DCS walk. The e2e injects the picture (coverage, Board and tracks), because the rig
  has no DCS.
- A real handoff accept, with a click on the target going to the server and its ack, was not walked
  end to end against real Strips. The unit test pins the dispatched op, and the e2e shows the
  resulting picture.
- The black background was not tried in light mode.

## For other lanes

- **F2:** no L22 hunk touches `hdgCorrection`, `app.js`, or any function F2 changes in `geojson.js`
  (`buildApproachVector`, `_extCenterline*`). ATC blocks draw only a typed assigned heading (`H250`),
  which is already magnetic, and the server's conformance `HDG###`. Neither converts a bearing, so no
  `toMagneticDisplay` call or stub was needed.
- **ERAM for CTR (later lane):** `scheme` would need a third value, or a client-side choice. The
  relation and click logic in `atc-scope.js` can be reused.
- **L28 (e2e lane 8):** the spec is `crc-desktop/e2e/l22-stars-scope.spec.js`. It writes into
  `docs/wip/L22/`.
