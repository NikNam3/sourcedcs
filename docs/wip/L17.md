## What the guide / briefing should say

`docs/efsp-usage-guide.md`, carrier section (build on L4's wip notes):

- The recovery Case is PriFly's, one setting for the ship; the other three Positions see it read-only. A weather
  advisory shows only when the set Case is less restrictive than the floor, and never blocks. Today the floor
  knows only night (the ship's ceiling and visibility are not sourced).
- The Marshal stack board: slot, flight, angels, DME, push time. The slot is the only number you set for a
  flight: drag a row (or the Strip) to the slot you want. Everyone above moves up together. Taking a flight out
  leaves a gap; press Close up on it if you want the stack to compress. Commence never moves anyone.
- Case I is an altitude list: type the squadron-assigned angels (2 to 20); there are no DME or push columns.
- Type the Charlie time as Zulu HHMM and the marshal radial as a MAGNETIC bearing; Enter sets, empty Enter on the
  radial returns to the default (final bearing + 180).
- Set EEAT (Zulu HHMM), approach, button, bingo and low state on the launch Strip; they are on the flight and are
  on the recovery Strip. The button is a number 1 to 20, never a frequency.
- Four hand-overs, four buttons: Commence (Marshal to a lane), Radar contact (lane, MARSHAL becomes FINAL), Ball
  (FINAL to BALL), See you (Case II only: to PriFly as PATTERN). Case I has its own "To pattern". Each records its
  trigger type. Dragging a Strip into a lane Bay does not do it; the button does.
- FINAL has nothing to type. A bolter is a drag to the Bolter Bay; the NLA brings it back on final.
- The banner is computed from DCS. Only the altimeter can be set. A `T` or `G` suffix means the variation or
  convergence is missing; "hull not found" and "ship track lost" are shown as they are.

`docs/efsp-briefing.md`: add `carrier-store.js` and `carrier-tick.js` to the module map; the Roles; the CARRIER

## Defaults taken (P2)

- **Q1 (a)** `LAUNCH` is correlation-eligible (reason above).
- **Q2 (a)** Block ids `C`-prefixed as 0064; `C11` (altimeter) and the ship WX are the banner, not Blocks.
- **Q3 (a)** FINAL's identity Blocks are `carrier-derived`, Block 5 off the map: nothing writable by construction.
- **Q4 (a)** An explicit `CarrierTransfer` op beside the NLA, one implementation.
- **Q5 (a)** A drag into a hand-over's Bay is refused with the button's name; a bolter is the one allowed drag.
- **Q6 (a)** A recovery check-in is appended to the stack at the next free slot; a full stack is a warning.
- **Q7 (a)** Radar selectors `{ kind: 'carrier', hull, radar }` as 0064 recommended: Marshal and lanes take both
  radars, PriFly the search radar.
- **Q8 (a)** Night is the sun more than 6 degrees below the horizon at the ship, from the mission clock.
- **Q9 (a)** Traffic count: a launch, a trap and a recovered pattern flight each count once.
- **Q10 (a)** Position letters `V`, `P`, `1`, `2` for the scope (`M` is taken by the tactical Positions).
- **Q11 (a)** The covering chain is 0064's (APP2 to APP1 to Marshal); the Marshal then holds Strips it cannot
  advance. Recorded in the ADR, not changed.
