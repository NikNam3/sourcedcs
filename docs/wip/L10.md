## What the usage guide should say

**What the colours mean.** A contact's colour is what *your* radars' interrogators got back, not the
game's idea of its side:

- **Blue (friendly)**: it reports on your datalink, or one of your tactical radars (AWACS, fighter,
  ship) got a valid Mode 4/5 reply. Only our own side has the keys.
- **Grey (neutral)**: it answered Mode 3/C (it squawks a code) but gave no valid Mode 4 reply, or
  your radars cannot ask Mode 4 at all (tower, approach and carrier approach radars cannot).
- **Yellow (bogey)**: nothing answered: silent, or a radar that asks nothing.
- **Orange (bandit) / red (hostile)**: only a controller's declaration.

Two controllers can see the same aircraft in different colours; each sees what their own
Positions' radars know. Holding both a tactical and an ATC radar, the tactical answer wins.

A declaration shows at once. An automatic change (a pilot switching Mode 4 on) shows with the next
radar sweep.

**For pilots: turn your Mode 4 on, or you are grey on the AWACS scope.** With no SRS client you have
no transponder at all and are a yellow primary return, unless your aircraft reports on the datalink.

**The tower sees every aircraft on the ground** within its radar's reach, including enemy ramps
(no ground hiding for now).

**Settings > DECLTR > Sequential squawk declutter** is now off by default. Turning it on hides the
labels of formation followers squawking sequential codes close to their lead.

## What the EFSP briefing should say

- `surveillance/iff.js` `classifyIff({declared, datalink, mode4, mode3})` is the one place a colour
  is chosen; it takes no track. `presentTrack` calls it per session. Automatic IFF never gives
  bandit/hostile.
- `Transponders#mode4Of(track)` (transponder.js): would the contact give a valid Mode 4/5 reply.
  Coalition is read only there (the crypto key), and in `transponderOf`/datalink participation.
- Radars carry `caps.mode4` (tactical yes, ATC no); `sensor-specs.json` `transponder.mode4For`.
- `describe()` returns `{who, transponder, mode4, iffOverride}`; no `iffState`. The label
  fingerprint in `ws-hub.js` holds the declaration only.
- There is no `invisible` state; aircraft on the ground are presented.
- `CLAUDE.md`'s crc-sync surveillance paragraph could add "IFF colour from `iff.js` `classifyIff`,
  per session (ADR 0066)".

  controller can still turn it back on.
