## What the usage guide should say

- **Opening METRICS.** Use PANELS → METRICS. The panel docks in the left cluster. Anyone signed in
  can open it, with or without a Position. It refreshes on open and every 30 s while it is on
  screen. It does nothing while it is hidden.
- **The two columns.** *This mission* is everything since the current DCS mission loaded. *Last
  hour* is a rolling 60 minutes. Use the selector to read back an earlier mission; it has no last
  hour.
- **The verdicts.**
  - `MET` / `NOT MET` compare the number with the §11.5 target. Only `NOT MET` is coloured.
  - `TRENDING ↓/→/↑` compares the last three complete hours, for the three metrics whose target is
    "trending down". It reads `NO TREND YET` until there are two complete hours.
  - `NO DATA` means the metric is measured, but nothing happened in this window.
  - `NOT INSTRUMENTED` means nothing measures this yet. It is not the same as zero. Staleness says
    `not instrumented (L19)` until its detector exists.
- **Search is a failure symptom (§4.3).** Every search is counted, but never what was searched for.
  A search count that is not trending down means controllers cannot find Strips where they expect
  them.
- **Time-to-find** runs from a Bay coming on screen to the first Strip you select in it. The clock
  ignores these:
  - a Bay you leave without selecting;
  - a Bay you hide;
  - a Bay you ignore for 10 minutes;
  - a click on the arrivals line.
- **Inputs per gesture** is a declared cost for each way a gesture is made. Double-click flip and
  Shift+click attention cost 1. Right-click + swatch highlight and ⋯ → Offset cost 2.
- **Per Position** (folded) lists search and time-to-find by Position. There is never a row per
  person.
- **Traffic count.** Pick a Facility.
  - The first line is a partition: every counted flight is local, transient or unknown.
  - Formation, SUA traversal and alert scramble are "of which" counts, and they can overlap.
  - "Reconciles with the Mutation log ✓" means the count agrees with a recount from the audit log.
    A mismatch is coloured.

## Findings

- **Gestures over the one-input ceiling (§7.3):** HIGHLIGHT costs 2 (right-click opens the swatch
  popover, a click picks a colour) and OFFSET costs 2 (open ⋯, click Offset). This is the finding,
  not a counting bug. The comment on `bay-view.js`'s `HIGHLIGHT_SWATCHES` says the swatch click
  "satisfies the one-input cost ceiling even though it's a popover". It does not count the
  right-click. **The supervisor decides:** either accept 2, or give both gestures a one-input entry
  point (a key, or a direct control), which would add a row to `GESTURE_INPUT_COST`.
- **Abandoned Bay entries during the e2e walk:** 2 (worked out from the steps, not read from the counters). The search pseudo-Bay was left for the OPS tab,
  and `ops-proposed` was left for `ops-coordination`. The empty `ops-coordination` Bay started no
  clock. One sample was taken (≈0.9 s, MET). Every walk-through left the arrivals-line exclusion at
  0 because the walk did not use the arrivals line (the unit tests cover it).
- **Metrics that never moved in the walk:**
  - correlation (no DCS, so no tracks);
  - transfers (none made);
  - staleness (L19).
  Search per manned hour read `—`: the walk was shorter than crc-sync's 60 s manning tick, so no
  manned minutes were recorded yet. That is honest, not a bug.
- **Events dropped stale or for overflow:** 0 in the walk. The counters are in
  `efspMetricsClientStats()`.
- **For LG / the integrator:** `crc-sync/tests/grpc-client-stream.test.mjs` "a stream that stayed
  up resets the backoff" failed once in a full `npm test` while the host load average was about 60.
  It passed 3/3 alone. It is timing-sensitive under load. I did not change it.
- **For L1 / the integrator:** `e2e/l1-popovers.spec.js` timed out in 3–8 tests at the 20 s budget
  under the same load. The same tests passed with `--timeout 90000`. They are not affected by
  L15's edits: the popovers are opened through menu items L15 did not touch, and the Offset item
  still returns `undefined`.
- No server bugs found. The contract matched.
