# L2 — MTR fields (guide §9.4) — lane notes

## Summary

This lane adds guide §9.4. There are six new Blocks on DEPARTURE, ARRIVAL and OVERFLIGHT, none on MISSION: `9G-MTR`, `9G-ENTRY` and `9G-TIME` (M10), and `9H-EXIT`, `9H-TIME` and `9H-ALT` (M11). Each is a plain `fdr` Block writing one leaf of `fdr.military.mtr`, with no interlock tag.

- **Validation on the server.** The designator and the fixes are free text, trimmed and upper-cased. The two times are typed as HHMM, stored as epoch ms and dated by the mission clock (S-L2a). The requested altitude after exit takes whatever `parseAltitudeFt` accepts.
- **Namespace table.** `MILITARY_BLOCK_NAMESPACE`'s M10/M11 rows now name the real Blocks (a `blocks` shape). The drift tests check them in both directions, and a guard fails if a wildcard row ever comes back.
- **Placement, as in the approved mockup (H51).** The MTR group is drawn on Layout C's field grid only when the flight has MTR data. It starts its own row at column 1, with M11 first. No colour is used.
- **Lost-comms rule.** It is shown as a grey note at the top of ▼ and as the hover title on the EXIT ALT label. It is never on the collapsed face.
- ADR `docs/adr/0062-mtr-fields-and-where-they-sit-on-the-strip.md` records all of the above.
- Mockup: `docs/wip/L2-mockup.html`.
- Screenshots: `docs/wip/L2/*.png`.
- Walk transcript: `docs/wip/L2/walk-notes.txt`.

## Defaults taken

- **S-L2a (supervisor), overriding the briefing's §5.3/Q2:** MTR times are stored as **epoch ms**, not `HHMM` strings. A typed `HHMM`, `HH:MM` or `…Z` is dated by the **mission clock** (the `FdrStore`'s injected clock, H11), never the wall clock, and is displayed as `HHMM`.
- **No reusable "typed HHMM → epoch" helper existed, so I wrote one:** `crc-sync/src/efsp/zulu-time.js`, a pure module with `parseZuluHhmm`, `resolveZuluHhmm(text, nowMs)` and `formatZuluHhmm(ms)`.
  - **Which day:** the occurrence nearest the mission's current time, within ±12 h. So `0010` typed at `2350Z` means tomorrow. This rule is `[SOURCE-DEFINED]`.
  - The client mirror is `strip-template.js`'s `formatZuluHhmm` plus `ZULU_HHMM_BLOCKS`. **L16 should reuse both** for the other typed time Blocks.
- A finite number written to an MTR time is taken as epoch ms already (imports, scenarios).
- **Q1/H25:** no history cell. The gap is recorded in the ADR and in the pilot walk below.
- **Q3/H24:** the briefing's per-Position table, `[SOURCE-DEFINED]`. A squadron member should confirm which Positions work MTR traffic.
- **Q4/H51:** the row is conditional, triggered when **any** of the six fields has a value, and sits on its own row with M11 first.
- **Q5:** clearing MTR does not clear the other five fields (no hidden writes).
- **Q6:** block altitudes (`FL190B210`) are refused; a single altitude only.
- **Q7:** the advisory is a grey ▼ note plus a `title` on EXIT ALT's label. Every MTR label has a short title (`blockTitleFor`).
- **Q8:** the designator and fixes are trimmed and upper-cased, with no format rule (D11, H23).
- **Q9:** posting MTR data counts as a filed-plan amendment (`updatedAt`). Scenario 5 asserts this.
- **ARRIVAL Block order:** the six sit after `9A-SPEED` and before `9E`, in the same place on both sides.

## What the usage guide should say

- **MTR fields (guide §9.4).** Any Strip can reach the six MTR Blocks from ▼: MTR, ENTRY, ENTRY TM, EXIT, EXIT EST and EXIT ALT.
- **When they appear on the Strip.** Once any of them has a value, they appear on the Strip as a row of their own:
  - DEP and OVF at APP and CTR: all six, in the order MTR · EXIT · EXIT EST · EXIT ALT · ENTRY · ENTRY TM.
  - ARR at APP and CTR: MTR plus the three exit fields.
  - OPS: MTR · ENTRY · ENTRY TM.
  - CD: MTR.
  - GND and TWR: none.
- **Clearing.** Clear every MTR field to remove the row. Clearing MTR alone leaves the rest.
- **Times.** Type them as `1432` or `14:32Z`. They are in-game Zulu, and the date is the mission's. Anything else is refused at the cell.
- **EXIT ALT.** It takes `FL210`, `080` or `8000`. It is the pilot's **request**. To approve it, write ALT. Only ALT is a clearance, and only ALT voids MARSA before rendezvous.
- **Lost-comms rule.** Open ▼, or hover the EXIT ALT label. It shows the ALT clearance in force and says that minimum IFR altitudes are not in the system.
- **Amending an MTR field overwrites it.** The previous value is not kept anywhere (see "Found, not fixed").

## What the briefing should say

- Blocks `9G-*`/`9H-*` are built (ADR 0062). `MILITARY_BLOCK_NAMESPACE` rows use either `blockId` or `blocks`, never both, and never a wildcard.
- `compactBlocksFor(role, positionId, fdr)`: the third argument adds the flight's conditional MTR group. The face (`strip-view.js`) and ▼ (`bay-view.js`) must pass the same FDR, or a Block ends up on both or on neither.
- Time Blocks: use `zulu-time.js` on the server and `ZULU_HHMM_BLOCKS`/`formatZuluHhmm` on the client. Don't store the typed string.
- A grey note in ▼ (`.efsp-expanded-note`) is the place for an advisory that isn't a warning.

## Pilot walk — "request a different exit fix"

I walked this as two controllers (CTR at CENTER, APP at INCIRLIK) in two browser contexts against the lane-2 crc-sync. The spec is `crc-desktop/e2e/mtr-pilot-walk.spec.js`, the verbatim observations are in `docs/wip/L2/walk-notes.txt`, and the screenshots are `docs/wip/L2/walk-*.png`. The flight is an inbound VIPER11 at CTR, handed off to APP.

1. **"VIPER11, IR107, exit F at 32, request FL190."**
   - CTR posted MTR from ▼. The row appeared on the face, and CTR filled EXIT, EXIT EST and EXIT ALT there.
   - APP's replica showed `IR107 F 1432 FL190` with no reload, as the M11 group after its normal fields. Works as designed.
2. **"Request exit at E instead, estimating 40, request FL210."**
   - APP overwrote the three fields, and CTR's Strip followed.
   - **Nothing on either Strip shows the old values or that anything was amended.** The old `F`/`1432`/`FL190` are on no struck entry, and they are not on the FDR. The Mutation log has no `op.value` either.
   - This is the §3.7 gap (H25), and it did feel wrong. "What was his old exit?" is a normal question in a handoff, and the board cannot answer it. **Recorded, not fixed.**
3. **"…approved FL210."** APP wrote ALT `FL210`. The ▼ note immediately read "…or the altitude in the last clearance: FL210." Works. The scenario (sortie 2) confirms the request did not void MARSA and the ALT write did.
4. **"Cancel IR107, direct home."**
   - Clearing MTR left EXIT, EXIT EST and EXIT ALT on the face: the trigger is any field.
   - **Helpful:** the stale exit data stays visible until it is dealt with, rather than hiding in ▼.
   - Clearing all of them removed the row.
5. **"Request IR109 instead."** Changing only the designator leaves exit fix `E`, which belonged to IR107, on the face with **no warning**. Nothing can know whether E is on IR109 until there is a route table (H23). **Recorded, not fixed.**
6. **Typo'd time `1472`.**
   - It was refused. The panel banner read `VIPER11 EXIT EST "1472" — MTR exit estimate must be a UTC time as HHMM, e.g. 1432`.
   - The cell reopened on `1472`, marked refused (F-207). Legible.
7. **APP's ARRIVAL.** The face carries only MTR plus the M11 group. ENTRY and ENTRY TM are not on it. As designed.
   - Incidental: while a refused cell is reopened as an input, that Block's `data-block` span is not in the DOM. That is pre-existing F-207 behaviour.
8. **CTR edits EXIT EST while APP is typing in EXIT.**
   - APP's open edit survived with its draft `D`, because the Strip is protected from rebuilds (ADR 0055).
   - APP's FDR copy already held 1445. The Strip only showed it once APP pressed Enter, and then showed both `D` and `1445`. No loss and no stale-rev refusal.
   - This is correct. The other controller's change is invisible while you type, which is the documented trade-off.

**Defects:** two, both recorded as out of this lane's scope, none fixed: (2) the amended value is kept nowhere, and (5) a stale exit fix after a designator change gets no warning. Nothing in this lane's files needed a fix.

## Found, not fixed

- **§3.7 overwrite gap for every plain `fdr` Block, the MTR fields included.** No history, and no `op.value` in the Mutation log. H25 schedules one fix for all of them. Owner: later slice; the candidate is a `clearance`-style FDR history cell.
- **No stale-exit-fix warning when the designator changes.** Owner: whoever lands the MTR route table (H23 / L16).
- **Typed time Blocks `6`, `14`, `14B`–`14D`, `16`–`18`, `M6`/`M7` still store the raw string** where readers expect epoch ms, and render the raw number (T9 / Q43). Owner: **L16** (§10.5 times). Reuse `zulu-time.js` and `ZULU_HHMM_BLOCKS`.
