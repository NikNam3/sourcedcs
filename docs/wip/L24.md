## What the usage guide should say

- A dropped flight stays on the server for **2 hours** after its drop, or until the **next
  mission** (a DCS mission start, a different mission loaded, or the mission clock jumping back more
  than 5 minutes), whichever comes first. Then it is archived: gone from every screen, from the
  server's memory and from the Board snapshot.
- Its history is the **Mutation log** (`state/efsp-mutations*.jsonl`). Each archive adds one
  `Archive` line per Strip and one `ArchiveFdr` line per flight record. There is no lookup in the
  app (H73).
- An archived drop cannot be undone. Undo after a mission change answers "not found".
- The traffic count is unaffected: every drop is counted before it can be archived.
