## What the usage guide should say

- The Strip's `TIMER` indicator (forwarding obligations — §4.6.1) **clears itself** once the
  obligation is no longer due: a void-expired flight re-cleared or released, a Strip forwarded, an
  airspace activated. There is nothing to acknowledge. A reconnect or reload shows only what is due
  now. The usage guide has no obligation section today (only the `TIMER` slot name at ~`:275` and the
  WP4A summary at `:10`); a short paragraph in §8 or §8E would do.
- `AMENDMENT_INSIDE_30MIN` shows for about one minute after the amendment, then clears (H38).
- `ADVANCE_FORWARDING` turns from amber (WARNING) to red (OVERDUE) when the ETA passes.

## What the briefing should say

`docs/efsp-briefing.md`:
- §3C, ~`:160–162`: "That is deliberately *not* the obligation-alert shape, which cannot retract"
  is now false — obligations retract too (0067), by the `efsp-alerts` full-state shape of `0058`.
- "Smaller, known, non-blocking", ~`:429–432`: the "server never retracts a forwarding-obligation
  alert" bullet goes (fixed by 0067).
- ~`:439`: "`recordMet()` on the obligation monitor still has no caller (WP8)" goes; it is called by
  the monitor for `ADVANCE_FORWARDING` and `VOID_TIME_EXPIRED`.

**For L5 (metrics)**: `obligationMonitor.getComplianceStats()` keeps its shape
`{ [type]: { met, missed } }`, but the meanings changed: `missed` = raised **episodes** (an
obligation that clears and comes due again counts twice); `met` = done before it was due, and only
ever non-zero for `ADVANCE_FORWARDING` and `VOID_TIME_EXPIRED`. The other four types are
missed-only by definition, so a compliance *rate* for them is meaningless — show counts. Counters
are unpersisted, reset on restart (unchanged from 0021).
