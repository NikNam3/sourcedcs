| T1 | **SFA does not follow the FAA model.** The `SFA` Position ("works ARRIVAL INBOUND") is ours: the FAA has no such Position. SFA is a procedure under a letter of agreement between the **radar approach controller** and the **GCA (PAR) controller**. JO 7610.14 ¶7-1-5: at least five discrete UHF frequencies (b), and *"the radar approach controller retains jurisdiction over the rotation of three frequencies between himself and the GCA controller"* (c). JO 7110.65BB ¶4-7-3: no frequency change after the approach begins until landing or low approach, except daylight VFR, pilot request, IFR cancelled, emergency, visual approach; ¶4-7-4 keep changes minimal below 2,500 ft. The build lets any of five racks be assigned among three Positions and does not know the exceptions. | `EFSPImplementationGuide.md` §4.7 (`[Coord §4.7]` = JO 7610.14 ¶7-1-5) was read as needing a third Position; ADRs 0075/0093 cite only the guide, never the orders | **Human (H128): the FAA model, always.** Drop the `SFA` Position; AP works the arrival on its SFA frequency and rotates it to PAR; three of the five frequencies rotate between AP and PAR; AP keeps the rotation. New ADR (supersedes ADR 0075/0093's SFA parts). Lane to be dispatched with the test's other fixes |
