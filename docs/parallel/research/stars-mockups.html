Today (tactical/GCI): for reference

What every scope draws now, TWR/APP/CTR included. Colour means affiliation (iffState), and every contact gets the same data block. Faithful to track-label.js, geojson.js buildLabels/buildDots/buildTrails/buildStcaOverlay and map-setup.js.

    Colours in use

    Why it doesn't serve ATC

    • It answers "whose side is it on?", but an ATC controller needs "what's my relation to it?" (mine / coming to me / pointed out / someone else's / nobody's). Ownership, handoffs and point-outs are nowhere on the scope, only on the Strips.
    • Every contact gets a full 3–4-line block, so a busy terminal area gets cluttered, and nothing orders the blocks by how much they matter to you.
    • * means radar height here, but on STARS * means pilot-reported. That's a false friend for anyone who knows STARS.
    • It stays exactly as it is for TAC_C2 / AIC / GCI, and it wins whenever a tactical radar sees the track (see Both radars).

    A · Strict STARS (7210.3 colour standard, TC-08/15 measured values)

    Black scope, dim-gray video map, blue fused targets with the owner's letter, and FDB/PDB/LDB chosen by your relation to each track. It carries no app extras: no conformance tags and no CPA overlay, because STARS has neither.

      Legend and sources

      Pros / cons

      • It's the most faithful option, and every colour traces to 7210.3 §3-9-1 or the measured STARS TCW values. STARS is also what a real USAF RAPCON uses, so it's correct for Incirlik APP in its own right.
      • Pure black gives white and green the most contrast, and the grading (FDB/PDB/LDB/none) removes most of the clutter.
      • A black scope hides the basemap (water, terrain, contours) that the rest of the app draws. On a mixed APP+GCI session the scope background would have to switch per session, not per track.
      • Saturated #00FF00 / #FF0000 are harsh on a desktop LCD next to the app's muted UI. You'd lose ADR 0058's conformance tags and CPA geometry, or have to keep them "off-standard" anyway.

      Needs from the server

      • A per-contact scheme: 'TACTICAL'|'ATC' in presentation.js (Q1). Without it the client can't apply "tactical wins".
      • Ownership, handoff and point-out states already exist (Strip ownerPositionId, coordination.*). The client resolves "mine" from the acting-as set. That's no server work.
      • New: a position letter per Position in facility config (Q5). Optional: a short coast hold for correlated tracks (Q6).

      B · STARS layout on the app's dark map (the research's "Proposed" column)

      Same grammar as A, with the same letters, blocks and the white/green/yellow/red meanings. It keeps the MapTiler dark basemap, softens the green, uses TC-08/15's recommended red, dims LDB/target/trail brightness, and keeps the app's ADR 0058 conformance tag and the CPA overlay as documented extras.

        Legend and sources

        Pros / cons

        • Anyone trained on STARS reads it as STARS, and it still sits on the same map as tactical tracks, so a mixed session doesn't need a background switch.
        • It keeps everything ADR 0058 built (the HDG/BUST tag, the CPA geometry), which STARS lacks and the squadron already uses.
        • Several values are approximations and have to be tested on the real map, as 7210 §3-9-1g–h requires of any implementation.
        • The amber conformance tag (#E0A83C) sits close to point-out yellow. The tag is always text on line 0, never a whole block, but check it on a real scope.
        • The softer green departs from the measured value, and white on a non-black map has less headroom than on black.

        Needs from the server

        • Same as A: scheme per contact (Q1), position letters (Q5), and optionally a coast hold (Q6).
        • No other wire change. Brightness categories (FDB/LDB/POS/OTH/PRI/HST/MP) are client-side opacity multipliers on existing layers.
        • Client: a monospace glyph stack (Roboto Mono) for MapLibre, a blue-disc icon and a second symbol layer for the letter (§7).

        C · ERAM-flavoured CTR (optional, only if CTR should feel like a Center)

        Here you act as CTR. ERAM codes by brightness, not by an ownership hue. It has its own symbol vocabulary (\ / + ◇ #), paired LDBs with no leader, assigned-vs-actual altitude in one field, and a 4th line for heading. The research (§4.2) recommends against this for v1. No FAA source gives ERAM colours, so the whole palette here is approximated from the vNAS rendering.

          Legend and sources

          Pros / cons

          • It feels like a Center: 060↓120 (assigned, trend, actual) in one field, H250 on line 4, EMRG/HIJK/RDOF in field E.
          • LDBs with no leader and no symbol are lighter, which suits CTR's wide theater-scale picture.
          • It breaks 7210.3 §3-9-1i ("one controller, one scheme"). A controller holding APP and CTR together, which is common, would see STARS and ERAM on one map, unless the scheme were chosen for the whole scope.
          • There's no FAA colour source, the symbol set is different, and it needs CIDs and sector numbers (new data). It's effectively a second ATC renderer.
          • CTR's radars are approach radars (2D+SSR, guide §8B), so nothing about the sensor picture calls for en-route presentation.

          Needs from the server

          • scheme would need three values (TACTICAL|STARS|ERAM), or ERAM gets chosen per session on the client.
          • New: a 3-character CID per flight (on the FDR) and a sector ID per Position for H22/O22.
          • Same ownership and coordination data as A/B. The CA "flash data block" needs no new data.

          Both radars: the H5 rule on one scope

          This controller holds GCI (sees an E-3's tactical radar) and APP (sees the LTAG approach radar). A track that a tactical radar currently sees is drawn tactical, even if the controller owns its Strip as APP. A track only the ATC radar sees is drawn STARS (variant B shown here).

            What the wire would carry (proposal, §5.1)

            // crc-sync presentation.js, per session, per contact
{ id: 17, scheme: 'TACTICAL',            // an E-3 (tactical type) sees it now
  sources: ['PRIMARY','SSR','HEIGHT'], label: { callsign: 'VIPER11', source: 'FDR' }, … }

{ id: 23, scheme: 'ATC',                 // only airport/approach radars see it
  sources: ['PRIMARY','SSR'],          label: { callsign: 'COLT31',  source: 'FDR' }, … }

{ id: 31, scheme: 'TACTICAL',            // E-3 lost it 1 sweep ago: held for ~2 sweeps
  sources: ['PRIMARY','SSR'], … }      //   (hysteresis), then flips to 'ATC'

            sources alone can't decide this. PRIMARY,SSR is what both kinds of radar report, so a contact the E-3 sees without height looks the same as one only APP sees. The radar type has to be classified once in sensor-specs.json (presentation: 'ATC' on airport and approach radars).

            Consequences to accept or reject (Q8)

            • An owned VIPER11 drawn tactical loses the STARS cues (white = mine, the A letter, PO/handoff blink) for as long as the E-3 sees it. The Strip still shows ownership.
            • Tracks flip scheme as AWACS coverage comes and goes. Hysteresis of about 2 tactical sweeps, and counting a flip as a label revision, keeps that calm but doesn't remove it.
            • A pure TWR/APP/CTR session never sees a tactical track: every contact is ATC.
            • The alternative is one scheme for the whole scope, picked by the "highest" Position held. That's simpler and obeys 7210's one-scheme rule, but a GCI+APP controller would then lose STARS entirely.

            What the owner must decide

            1. Which variant: A (strict), B (STARS on the app map, the research's proposal), or B with C added later for CTR?
            2. scheme on the wire: should crc-sync add scheme: 'TACTICAL'|'ATC' per contact, from the radar types that currently see it, with about 2 sweeps of hysteresis? (§5.1, §9 Q1)
            3. Mixed sessions: do you accept per-track mixing with flips, or pick one scheme for the whole scope from the highest Position held? (Q8)
            4. ADR 0058 extras on ATC tracks: should the conformance tag and CPA overlay stay (B) or go (A)?
            5. Position letters for TWR/APP/CTR, and the owner letter after TOFI hands a flight to a tactical Position. (Q3, Q5)
            6. Coast: should a correlated track hold CST for about 2 sweeps (new server or client state), or should the Strip's NO TRK be enough? (Q6)
            7. Primary-only symbol: + (shown here, matches ERAM) or the vNAS diamond? (Q7)
            8. Declared hostiles on ATC-scheme tracks: show nothing (pure H5), or a yellow SA-style indicator? (Q2)
