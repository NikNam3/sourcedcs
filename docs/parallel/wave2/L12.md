## 2. Mission

The guide (`EFSPImplementationGuide.md` §9.5, whole):

> `M14` ∈ `{CLEAN, LOADED, HUNG, EXPENDED}`.
> `HUNG` MUST propagate to field state: verified local practice is that hung ordnance influences
> **runway selection** — Kunsan selects the runway that minimises taxi distance to the hot cargo
> pad, weather permitting `[Annex §13.8]`. Implement as an advisory on runway assignment plus a
> routing constraint toward the designated hazardous-cargo parking area.

The WP6 plan (Phase 4) settles the reading: an **advisory, not an inhibit**; the hot-cargo pad from
the field-state config is the named routing constraint; the Kunsan basis is **local practice at one
base, not doctrine**; **no taxi-distance computation** (no geometry exists). **H21** then settled the
data: the pads are **placeholders, names only, no preferred direction; the advisory states the fact
without recommending a runway.**

**Done means:**

1. A pure `hungOrdnanceAdvisoryFor(...)` appended to crc-sync's `src/efsp/field-state.js` (L1's pure
   module) returning the advisory for a flight whose `fdr.military.ordnanceState === 'HUNG'`, or
   `null`. It names the resolved runway (as a fact, not a recommendation) and the configured hot
   cargo pad, and says plainly when none is configured.
2. A client mirror `ordnanceAlertsFor(strip)` in a new `panels/efsp/ordnance-advisory.js`, rendered
   through the wave-2 hook lines (§7.3) as an `ORD`-slot chip `HUNG` plus a reason line, on every
   Position's view of the Strip. Held to the server function by a drift test.
3. The routing constraint rendered: the reason line tells the controller the aircraft goes to the
   hot cargo pad **by name**, `[SOURCE-DEFINED]`, "SOURCE practice".
4. Tests in both packages; a Playwright check on `E2E_LANE=3` with screenshots in `docs/wip/L12/`;
   the pilot requests of §6 step 4 walked and written up.
5. ADR `0069`; `docs/wip/L12.md`.

**Out of scope:** any inhibit or refusal; taxi-distance or runway recommendation (H21); pad
occupancy ops (`hotCargoPad.occupied` stays present-and-unpopulated per §12 — Q3); any store,
wire-message or `efsp-ws.js` change; any change to Block `3G` (built, ADR 0052) or to who may set
it (Q2); `LOADED`/`EXPENDED` behaviour (Q8); the field-state panel (L1b's).

## 9. Traps
