## 2. Mission

The guide (`EFSPImplementationGuide.md` §9.6, whole):

> `M16` ∈ `{NONE, ALERT, SCRAMBLE}`.
> **Alert status is a ground-movement constraint at least as much as an air constraint**. Verified
> local rules: taxiing aircraft yield to alert scrambles; aircraft must not block runway access from
> the alert pad; a specific taxiway is kept clear for alert scrambles.
> Implement: a `SCRAMBLE` Strip MUST raise a Board-wide priority indication, and MUST mark the
> configured alert-pad access route as constrained, with any conflicting taxi Strip flagged.
> **[GAP] Do not implement a scramble/interceptor priority *ordering* from this guide.** FAA JO
> 7110.65 §2-1-4 and §9-2-7 were not read. Until then, `SCRAMBLE` raises an indication and the
> controller decides.

**Done means:**

1. `M16` gets its Block: **`14E`** on `DEPARTURE_BLOCK_MAP` (Q1), `kind: 'military', field:
   'alertStatus'`, routed through the existing `setMilitary()` by the generic `SetBlock` path (a
   logged Strip Mutation — which is what L5's traffic count needs). `MILITARY_BLOCK_NAMESPACE.M16`
   names it; the drift tests check it; the client mirrors it (label `ALERT`, an enum `<select>`
   `NONE`/`ALERT`/`SCRAMBLE`) and places it on the per-Position grid (Q2).
2. A **Board-wide priority indication**: a line at the top of the Strip panel, visible in every
   Position tab, naming each flight currently scrambling, its Facility and state, the alert-pad
   access route that is constrained, and how many ground Strips are flagged. Clicking a name selects
   that Strip.
3. **The alert-pad access route marked constrained**: on that line, and in L1b's field-state panel
   through the `alertPadConstraintFor(facilityId)` hook (§7.3). Read from config (`[SOURCE-DEFINED]`,
   H21 placeholder names).
4. **Conflicting ground Strips flagged**: every live Strip at the scrambling flight's Facility in a
   ground state (Q3) gets a `SCRAMBLE` chip and a reason line — **no inhibit, no reordering, no
   automatic sequencing, no transfer** (the `[GAP]`, binding). The scrambling Strip itself gets its
   own chip.
5. `nla.js`'s `CLEARED` placeholder comment rewritten to say why this is a **flag, not an inhibit**,
   with a test proving `computeNla` is unchanged by a scramble.
6. Pure rules in a new crc-sync module (`src/efsp/alert-scramble.js`), a client mirror
   (`panels/efsp/scramble.js`) held to it by a drift test, tests in both packages, a sortie, a
   Playwright walk with screenshots in `docs/wip/L13/`, ADR `0070`, `docs/wip/L13.md`.

**Out of scope:** any priority ordering, queue reordering, auto-move, auto-transfer or inhibit
(`[GAP]`); a taxi-route model (none exists — write down the narrowing one would allow, do not fake
it); `M17` fuel state; alert-pad **occupancy** ops (the record's `alertPad {occupied, occupantFdrId}`
stays present-and-unpopulated, §12 — Q7); any change to L1's store/wire; an automatic reset of
`alertStatus` (Q4); sound.
