ABOUT THE SQUADRON

What is SOURCE?

SOURCE is a DCS World virtual aviation squadron dedicated to realistic, coordinated tactical aviation. We operate across multiple airframes with an emphasis on combined-arms missions, structured briefings, and open-source tooling that any sim pilot can use or contribute to.

⊕
TACTICAL OPERATIONS
Full ATO/ACO/SPINS packages, professional briefings, and coordinated combined-arms execution across multiple airframes.
◉
OPEN-SOURCE TOOLS
All our tactical tools — ATO Brief, ASACS GCI server, Miz-to-YAML — are fully open source and free to use.
≡
REALISTIC PROCEDURES
We follow real-world-inspired procedures: proper comms discipline, ROE, SPINS, ACO, and mission planning cycles.
⌥
WELCOMING COMMUNITY
Whether you're new to DCS or a veteran simmer, SOURCE has a place for motivated, team-first pilots.

JOIN SOURCE

How to Join

SOURCE welcomes motivated DCS pilots committed to realistic, coordinated operations. No previous virtual squadron experience required — just a good attitude, a working mic, and a willingness to follow procedures.

01
SUBMIT AN APPLICATION
Fill in the short form with your callsign, timezone, and preferred wing.
02
JOIN DISCORD
We'll reach out on Discord within 48 hours to introduce you to the squadron.
03
FLY WITH US
Join a training sortie or observer slot to get familiar with SOURCE procedures and comms.
04
BECOME A MEMBER
After evaluation you'll be formally inducted, assigned a callsign, and get access to all ops.
REQUIREMENTS
  • DCS World installed (at least one full airframe module)
  • Working headset and microphone
  • SRS (Simple Radio Standalone) installed
  • Discord account
  • Commitment to attend at least one op per month
  • Respectful, team-first attitude
Age 16+ · English required for comms
