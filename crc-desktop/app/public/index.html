Formation Label Declutter

    

      

        Sequential squawk declutter
        
      

    

    

      When aircraft squawks are sequential (e.g. 1101, 1102, 1103) and each
      aircraft is within 0.5 nm horizontally and
      1 000 ft vertically of the previous,
      labels for followers are hidden. Lead aircraft (1101) always shows its label.
    


    

    
Navpoint Declutter

    

      

        Hide numbered navpoints
        
      

      

        5-letter names only
        
      

    

    

      Numbered: hides navpoints whose names contain digits (e.g. WP1, NAV003).

      5-letter: hides navpoints whose names are not exactly 5 characters (e.g. KARAX, DOSAD).
